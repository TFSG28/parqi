## Design Context

### Users
Drivers using the mobile app while looking for parking in Portugal. The primary job is to find a trustworthy nearby place quickly, understand the map at a glance, and open directions or details without navigating a busy dashboard.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, confident, practical, and community-aware. The interface should reduce cognitive load and help drivers feel oriented and in control, with clear Portuguese (PT-PT) copy and restrained status cues.

### Aesthetic Direction
Map-first mobile product inspired by the supplied dark navigation-map reference: full-screen cartographic surface, subtle floating controls, high-contrast blue parking clusters, and a compact search affordance. Follow the device theme for the rest of the app, while keeping the map visually calm and legible in both light and dark modes. Use the existing Parqi road-sign identity, Barlow typography where already established, and avoid decorative cards, gradients, or excessive chrome.

### Design Principles
- Let the map be the hero and keep controls contextual and compact.
- Prioritize one fast path: search, filter, select a place, then navigate.
- Use blue for trusted availability and orange only for action or attention.
- Preserve orientation with visible location and clear map state feedback.
- Keep touch targets accessible, copy concise, and motion restrained.
